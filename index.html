What you get

  • Ad & tracker blocking

    OrbitX Shield blocks ads and trackers on the sites you visit.

  • No accounts

    Nothing to sign up for. Your bookmarks and history stay on your device.

  • No telemetry

    No usage data and no browsing history is sent to the developer.

  • HTTPS-only & fingerprint protection

    Optional HTTPS-only mode, and protection against browser fingerprinting.

  • Private windows

    Nothing is saved to history, and cookies are dropped when the last private window closes.

  • Built to stay light

    Tabs you are not using go to sleep to free memory.
