OrbitX for iOS – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026. Applies to OrbitX Browser for iPhone (App Store version). The Windows version has its own privacy policy.

In short

OrbitX has no accounts, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no third-party SDKs. Nothing is sent to the developer, and the developer runs no server that could receive your data. In App Store terms: Data Not Collected. Your history, bookmarks, settings and passwords stay on your iPhone. OrbitX does make a few network requests on its own (filter lists, site icons, and optional features you switch on). They go directly from your iPhone to the party named below and contain no account, device identifier or usage statistics. This page lists each one.

2. Requests OrbitX makes itself

All of these go straight from your iPhone to the named party, over HTTPS, without cookies. The other party necessarily sees your IP address, as with any web request. OrbitX does not check for its own updates; app updates come through the App Store.

3. Websites you visit

When you open a website or search, your browser exchanges data with that site or with your search engine (DuckDuckGo by default), as every browser does. OrbitX's shield blocks many ad and tracker requests. When you download a file, the request to the file's server carries the page's cookies, so that downloads behind a login work; this is ordinary browser behaviour. What a website does with your data is governed by that website's own policy.

4. Camera, microphone and location

OrbitX only uses the camera, microphone or location when a website asks for them. You are asked for each website, and you can change your choice in the site settings. The iOS permission is requested only after you allow a website. OrbitX itself does not record, store or send this data; it goes only to the website you allowed.

5. What is stored on your iPhone

History, bookmarks, shortcuts, open tabs, settings, per-site settings, the list of downloads, site icons, filter lists and saved passwords are stored on your iPhone and are never uploaded by OrbitX. There is no sync. The app's data folder (history, passwords, downloads list, site icons, news cache, filter lists) is excluded from iCloud and device backups.

Saved passwords

Saved passwords are encrypted with AES-256-GCM. The key is created on your iPhone and kept in the iOS Keychain, available only while the device is unlocked and never leaving this device. Viewing or filling a password requires Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode. Passwords are offered only on the exact website (origin) where they were saved. If you export a backup, the file is encrypted with a password you choose; where you keep that file is up to you. A copied password is removed from the clipboard after 30 seconds and is not shared with your other devices.

Private tabs

Private tabs do not save history, search suggestions, passwords, site icons or tabs. Cookies and site data from private tabs are kept in memory only, shared only between private tabs of the same session, and discarded when you close the last private tab. When you switch away from the app during a private session, the screen is covered so that the app switcher snapshot does not show the page. Files you download stay on your iPhone.

Deleting your data

Use Settings > Privacy and security > "Clear browsing data", or delete individual entries in History, Bookmarks, Downloads and the password vault. Deleting the app removes all of its data.

6. Legal basis and your rights

The requests in section 2 are needed to provide the features you use (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR); the optional ones are off until you switch them on. Since the developer holds no personal data about you, requests for access, correction or deletion would normally have nothing to act on. You may nevertheless contact the address above at any time. You have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority, for example the Landesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz und die Informationsfreiheit Rheinland-Pfalz.

7. Children

OrbitX is a general-purpose browser and is not directed to children. It does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

8. Changes

If a feature changes what is requested or stored, this page will be updated before the change is released and the date at the top will change.
